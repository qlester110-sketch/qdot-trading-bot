## Out-of-universe override rules

Override allowed only with all of the following:
1. Owner explicitly approves before entry (not after)
2. Written justification in today's RESEARCH-LOG entry
3. Trade still passes the standard buy-side gate (catalyst, falsification, materiality ≥ 0.6, direction, horizon)
4. Override is tagged in TRADE-LOG entry as `out_of_universe: true` for post-hoc tracking

Frequent overrides defeat the point of having a universe — if more than
2 out-of-universe trades happen in a calendar month, the universe gets
re-evaluated at the next weekly review.
